These Are the Gifts Developers Will Actually Want

Some people tell you exactly what they want for the holidays, but some people just don’t. The gifts, the decor, the food, for all of it, you have to guess at what they might like. You trawl thousands of gift guide lists titled “the definitive gift guide for 2026,” but none of the guides are quite right for that special person. That is, until this one came along.

As long as that person in your life is a developer.

We won’t say it’s the definitive guide for the developer in your life (or for yourself, we won’t judge), but we have brought together a few of our favorite pieces from the GitHub Shop for you to pick from.

Shop the gift guides

Get building

At GitHub Universe this year we brought our brand new GitHub x Omnitype Bauer Lite Keyboard Kit to the Makerspace for our attendees to build. To say it was popular is an understatement. If you like building keyboards, you’ll want the details. Each GitHub-exclusive Bauer Lite kit arrives in a randomly selected color combination and includes a hot-swap PCB with RGB underglow, Omnitype Hawk linear switches, dye-sublimated PBT keycaps, and the essential hardware and tools for a hands-on custom build. And if you’re new to building keyboards then you can follow along with our step-by-step guide.

If you like building of a different variety, perhaps our new GitHub jigsaw puzzle will be calling you. Grab yourself a hot beverage, settle in, and start building. Speaking of which…

Busy builders gift guide

Get cozy

If the preference is to work on the sofa in ultimate comfort, then this is the gift guide for you. Drape a cozy blanket over you, put on some warm socks, and while your agent is busy at work you can slip on the sleep mask and have a little mid-afternoon nap.

Cozy coders gift guide

Get decorating

If the desk of you or your loved one is looking a bit dull, brighten it up with a colorful desk mat, selection of mugs and tumblers, and two buddies to help with all your coding decisions. The Copilot Amazeball sits on your desk until you have a burning question. Should you ship on a Friday? Give the Amazeball a shake, turn it over, and get your answer. Your other tiny teammate, the Mona Octobobble, will simply nod along to all your best ideas. Of which, we’re certain, there are many.

Desk decorators gift guide

Get traveling

If your January resolution is to become a digital nomad then there are gifts here for you, too, especially if you have all the ideas but no gear. From tech organizers to work totes to awesome skateboards, we’ll get you where you need to go in style.

Well-traveled devs gift guide

Super fans unite

Super fans, check out some of our most popular items from the shop, like our classic invertocat hoodie. Plus get your hands on the matching version for your drink.

Cold outside? Then dress up your outfit with our Mona Varsity jacket. Too warm for that? Then you’ll love our Cabana shirt and shorts co-ord.

GitHub super fans gift guide

Something for the next generation

Whether it’s for a brand-new baby or the tweenager just getting into coding, we have something for all ages. Start them off with the Invertocat onesie and move on into the matching Mona bestie bottle and Mona plush.

Next-gen devs gift guide
